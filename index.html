Learn About Our Criteria

Every chair below is scored against Kanishka's 4 must-haves.

1

Maintaining the "S" (Lumbar)

Lumbar support adjustable in depth & height, plus support for the mid-back.

2

Adjustable Armrests

Arms adjust to keep shoulders relaxed and wrists neutral.

3

Seat Depth

Extendable enough to support the whole thigh — no edge digging into the upper calves.

4

Height Adjustable

Lets your heels stay flat on the ground.

5

Head Support (Neck a bonus)

Head support is a must; neck support is an added benefit.

6

Foam-Based Seat

Cushioned foam seat rather than hard mesh for long sitting.

7

Flexible Tilt

Forward & backward tilt for proper back support.
